# Product detail page update

- Every product card can now be opened by clicking its image, title, view action, or unused card area.
- Product routes use `/products/<product-slug>` for all eight listed products.
- Product detail pages now include a marketplace-style product image area, category breadcrumbs, pack-size choices, quantity control, add-to-cart and buy-now actions, WhatsApp enquiry, delivery enquiry by PIN, product highlights, product information tabs, cooking-use guidance, related products, more products, and FAQs.
- The product detail layout is responsive and includes reduced-motion support.
- Only one supplied product image per product was available, so the page explicitly notes that more product photographs can be added; it does not claim to have verified alternate photos.
- Prices, stock and delivery serviceability are not fabricated. The customer is told to confirm price and delivery availability with the business.

Run the existing project with `npm install` and `npm run dev`.
